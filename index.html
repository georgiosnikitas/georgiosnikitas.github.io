About this business

This is an independent, one-person software studio run by Georgios Nikitas. Products are designed, built, and supported personally.

What is sold: Small, self-contained software products. Some are downloadable desktop utilities (such as Brain Break), others are lightweight web/SaaS applications (such as Auto Garage).

How they are made and licensed: Each product is developed in-house. Buyers receive a non-transferable license to use the software for the intended scope (single user for personal tools, single small business for business tools). Source code stays under the developer's ownership.

Who they are for: Individuals looking for simple personal-productivity tools, and small businesses (such as independent auto garages) that need a straightforward, affordable management tool without the complexity or recurring cost of larger platforms.

Billing model: All current products are single one-time purchases. No subscriptions are offered today. If a subscription product is ever introduced, it will be clearly listed on this page with its own pricing.
